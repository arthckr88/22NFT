# Summary

## Placement, in order

1. **A bolted, sealed tail enclosure for the two B4810 packs** (option b). It is the only idea that keeps the bank out of sight and out of the cabin and still satisfies the battery manual’s keep-dry rule. It is first only if the tape shows a gap and the yellow sticker still has rear GAWR margin after the lever-arm math.
2. **Split the packs, one aft and one near the axle** (option e). At the published chassis frame end (37.35 in behind the axle) the split adds about 255 lb to the rear axle instead of about 283 lb with both packs and the hub at that station.
3. **Both packs between the rails, on the rear axle** (option d). The rear then sees the 234 lb and the front sees none of it, if the unmeasured gap is actually empty.

Bare packs beside the spare, a hitch box, and roof-mounted batteries are ruled out. The hitch is the Fiat’s tow bar. An open spare well is a spray zone, and the B4810 manual says not to use the pack in water.

## Biggest risks

- **CCC is unknown.** East to West defines it and does not publish it. The yellow sticker wins.
- **The partial parts stack is already large.** RV5 30.86 lb + two B4810 202.8 lb + one Orion 4 lb + Splendide 148 lb + six panels. Panels are **393.9 lb** on Callsun’s page or **178.8 lb** on a conflicting listing. Those sums are **779.6 lb** or **564.5 lb**, before the box, the desk, mounts, cables, Starlink (model open), tow gear, water (8.3 lb/gal), and personal gear.
- **GCWR headroom at a full GVWR coach is 2,500 lb** (13,500 − 11,000). Published Abarth curb weights are 2,491 lb and 2,512 lb. A loaded coach plus the car can miss GCWR by a small amount. Weigh both.
- **The air conditioner is published as soft-start and ducted, not as variable-speed.** That may be a single-speed compressor with a soft start. Confirm before money changes hands.
- **Six panels in parallel on one RV5 port are 82.14 A Isc, over the 50 A limit.** Two in series are 50.52 V at STC, over the 50 V limit. The array that fits the published limits is two parallel groups of three, one per PV port, and even that is tight if a 125% current factor applies (51.3 A). AWAITING BLUETTI.
- **Rear overhang, departure angle, tank locations, and the spare hanger are unpublished.** A tail box drawn before those tapes can cover a dump, an exhaust, or the spare.

## Weight versus CCC

**CCC unknown.** Do not treat 779.6 lb or 564.5 lb as a cargo result. They are the sourced parts only, and they disagree by the panel-weight conflict.
