# Vehicle specs — East to West Alita 22NFT / 22NF

Accessed 2026-10-06. Every number below is tied to a source in [SOURCES.md](../SOURCES.md) and in [data/vehicle.json](../data/vehicle.json). The VIN yellow sticker on the actual coach overrides published UVW, CCC, and axle weights. Those sticker values were not available.

## What the name is

East to West’s current floorplan page is the **2027 Alita 22NF**. A dealer inventory page still lists a **2027 Alita 22NFT** and marks that listing discontinued. RV Guide indexes both a **2026 22NF** and a **2027 22NF**, and says the model was introduced in 2026. No separate manufacturer spec sheet titled “22NFT” was found with its own numbers. Treat 22NFT as the earlier designation of this floorplan until a factory document says otherwise.

Example dealer VINs published with 2027 coaches: `1FDRU8PG6RKB65044` and `1FDRU8PGXRKB68951`. The 10th VIN character is **R**, which is the 2024 model-year code. Coach model year and chassis model year are not the same thing. Confirm both on the unit you buy.

## Weights and ratings

From the manufacturer floorplan page:

| Rating | Value |
| --- | --- |
| GVWR | 11,000 lb |
| GCWR | 13,500 lb |
| GAWR front | 4,630 lb |
| GAWR rear | 7,275 lb |
| Published tow capacity | 4,000 lb |

East to West defines UVW and CCC on that page and states that each coach is weighed and labeled before it ships. **They do not publish a UVW or a CCC for the 22NF.** Both are unknown until the yellow sticker is read. Dealer tables that say Dry Weight N/A and Cargo Capacity N/A match that gap.

GCWR minus GVWR is **2,500 lb**. That is the combined-weight headroom if the coach is loaded all the way to GVWR. It is less than the 4,000 lb tow figure. East to West also says the actual GCWR of the vehicle may be limited by GVWR plus the installed hitch receiver’s rating, and to read the hitch label. A Fiat 500 Abarth sits near that 2,500 lb line (see below). The car’s weight counts against GCWR. It does not, by itself, count against the coach CCC. Weight the tow bar puts on the receiver does.

UVW, the as-built front axle weight, and the as-built rear axle weight: **not published.**

Ford’s 2023 Transit Body and Equipment Mounting Manual, in the EL-LWB 178 in column, lists:

- Rear axle to end of frame, not including the frame adapter: **37.35 in**
- Maximum rear frame extension: **80 in**
- Those two add to **117.35 in** from the axle to the end of a maximum extension

That is the **chassis frame**, not the motorhome body. A Class C body can extend past the frame. It is recorded so the tail enclosure can be compared with the frame that actually exists. It is not a measurement of an Alita.

A Kelderman drawing for a 2014+ Transit 350HD DRW cab chassis and cutaway labels **CTC Frame 35.400 in**. That is center-to-center of the rails, on that vendor’s drawing, not the inside clear gap, and not confirmed on an Alita. Inside width is a field measurement (D-02, D-04).

Fresh water is **8.3 lb/gal** in East to West’s own CCC definition. A full fresh tank is 35 × 8.3 = **290.5 lb**, and that weight is part of CCC, not part of UVW.

## Generator, spare, batteries, hitch

- **Generator.** The floorplan says a 4,000 W generator and does not name the brand or the bay. One Parris RV 2027 unit (stock ATB68951) lists **Flex Power 4000**. That is credible for that listing, not proof that every 22NF uses it. The RVMP Flex Power 4000i manual lists the machine as 29.5 × 19.25 × 13.5 in and **117 lb** with oil and without the hanging frame; the product page lists **121 lb**. Neither figure is the coach’s installed weight or the bay’s location. Bay location: UNVERIFIED.
- **Spare.** RV Guide lists a spare as standard. Location and mounting method: not published. Tire size on RV Guide conflicts: **195/75R16C** on the 2026 page, **205/75R16C** on the 2027 page. Read the tire placard.
- **Factory batteries.** The solar option is a 200 W panel, a 30 A controller, and **two 12 V batteries in the step well**. That is the published house-battery location for the option. It is not a description of a B4810 install. Whether a given coach has the option is a sticker question.
- **Hitch.** RV Guide lists a rear receiver. East to West publishes **4,000 lb tow capacity** and tells you to read the hitch label. Hitch class and tongue rating were not published. Dealer tables that say “Hitch Weight 4000 lbs” next to GVWR are repeating the tow number in the wrong column. Tongue rating: UNVERIFIED.

## Flat-tow context (the Fiat, not the coach)

Fiat’s 2017 500 Abarth owner’s manual allows flat towing a **manual** transmission with all four wheels down, transmission in neutral, up to **65 mph**. The 2013 and 2015 manuals allow a manual to be flat towed at any legal highway speed. Automatics are not allowed flat. The car’s year is OPEN, and the speed limit depends on it.

Curb weight: a 2013 Fiat Canada press kit lists **2,512 lb** for the 5-speed Abarth. A compilation of the NHTSA Canadian vehicle-spec database lists **2,491 lb** for 2015–2019 Abarth hatchbacks. Both sit on top of the **2,500 lb** GCWR headroom that remains when the coach is at GVWR. Weigh the coach and the car together before treating the combo as inside GCWR. Base plate, tow bar, supplemental brake, and lighting weights were not sourced; they land on the coach and come out of CCC.
