# Interior layout

Washer location stays **OPEN**. Desk layout stays a set of options. No cabinet was dimensioned in a factory drawing, so this file does not invent cabinet widths.

## What the interior is, from sources

Confirmed or credible zones, front to back, from the manufacturer list and the Parris walkthrough:

| Zone | What is published | What a solo build might touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cab | Swivel driver and passenger seats | Leave them. They are the driving position and a lounge |
| Cabover | 60 × 80 in bunk with a cargo net | Storage for a solo occupant, or a guest bunk. Not required for the office |
| Dinette | “Booth dinette” in dealer copy. Size not published | Could become work surface. That is a different desk than a rear office |
| Kitchen | 6 cu ft 12 V fridge, induction cooktop, convection microwave, deep sink, counter extension | Leave the working kitchen. A full-timer still cooks |
| Bath | Shower, sink, toilet. RV Guide says center. No sizes | Option A for the washer |
| Slide | Exists (slide box, slide toppers). Wall and length unpublished | Do not plan the washer into the slide until the wall is known |
| Rear | Dealer: 60 × 80 in queen **lift** bed. Cavity under a lift bed is implied and unmeasured | Option B for the washer, and the rear office |

RV Guide lists washer/dryer prewire as **no**. A Splendide is new work: a 15 A dedicated circuit, hot and cold (or cold only, depending on the install), a 1-1/4 in standpipe whose top is 25–34 in above the machine’s bottom, and either a metal dryer duct (WD2100XC) or no duct (WDC7100XC).

## Washer — option A, bath side

| Topic | What is known |
| --- | --- |
| Weight | 148 lb. Splendide says put an RV install over the axles or midship. A center bath is the closer match to that sentence, if the bath really is centered. “Center” is an RV Guide flag, not a measured station |
| Plumbing | Bath already has supply and a drain. Run length should be the short one. Gray tank is 30 gal and its location is unknown, so “short” still has to be confirmed with a tape |
| Vent | Vented model: a duct through the nearby sidewall, metallic, short, not into the underbelly. Ventless model: no duct, and about 2.5 gal/hour of dry time plus 7.5–16 gal for the wash, out of a 35 gal fresh tank |
| Storage lost | The bath cabinet or a closet that has not been dimensioned. Cannot be stated in cubic feet |
| Noise | The machine is next to the shower and, on a 26 ft floor, not far from the rear desk. A 1,200 rpm spin will be heard at the desk either way. The bath wall may be the better sound barrier. That is a guess about walls that have not been seen |
| Floor | Must support 280 lb. Confirm the bath floor is not a plumbing chase with no structure under the footprint |

## Washer — option B, rear garage / under the lift bed

| Topic | What is known |
| --- | --- |
| Weight | Same 148 lb, and it lands at the rear. Added to a tail battery box, it stacks more load on the long lever. The axle formulas in the underbelly note apply with \(W = 148\) once \(x\) is measured. This is the main reason not to assume the rear is free |
| Plumbing | A new run from the bath manifold or from the pump to the rear, and a standpipe. Longer, more fittings, more to freeze. The Arctic package heats tanks, not a washer supply you add |
| Vent | A rear-wall duct is short if the machine sits against the back cap. It can also punch the cap next to the camera or the ladder. Ventless avoids that and spends fresh water |
| Storage lost | The cavity under the lift bed, which is also the natural garage for chairs, a table, and bulk goods. A solo rear office that needs that cavity for the desk collides with a washer in the same cavity |
| Noise | The spin is in the office. Headphones and a closed door help only if there is a door. No rear-room door is published |
| Splendide’s own RV line | “Over the axles or midship” argues against the extreme rear. Not an absolute ban. It is the manufacturer’s motion warning |

Side-by-side conclusion, without a pick: the bath is the better match to Splendide’s placement sentence and to existing plumbing, and it spends an unknown cabinet. The rear is the better match to “keep the bath a bath,” and it spends garage volume and rear-axle margin in the same place the batteries want. Measure both footprints before choosing. The machine’s box is about **23.4 in wide, 33–34 in high, 22–22.6 in deep**, with 1 in of case clearance front and back in the data sheet and more if a door has to clear the control panel.

## Rear office

Three ways to get a desk at the back. None is selected.

1. **Lift bed stays, desk slides out under it.** Uses the lift. Headroom under a 60 × 80 bed is unpublished, so a seated desk may not fit. This is the one to mock up with cardboard on a dealer unit.
2. **Lift bed removed or left up, desk on the rear wall.** A full-time solo coach can give up the rear bed and keep the cabover for guests. Frees the whole rear width, whatever that width is (exterior width is TBD, so interior width is unknown). Best work surface, worst resale and worst guest story.
3. **Desk beside a bed that stays down.** A 60 in bed across a coach whose outside width is unpublished may leave no aisle. Do not draw this as a fit.

Monitor and editing gear: power comes from the RV5 AC output (5,000 W, 120 V). The circuit for a workstation should be a normal branch, not the washer’s 15 A circuit. Watts of the workstation are an input in the planner, not a published coach load.

## Where the RV5’s panel goes

The hub can live underbelly. The thing you look at is the **Epad** (and the hub’s own LCD, which you will not see if the hub is outside). Epad dimensions were not in the spec table extracted. Place the Epad at the desk or just inside the entry, on a wall you can see without opening a bay. Cable length from a tail hub to a rear desk is the short run; from a tail hub to a cab display is the long one. Communication is Ethernet in the RV5 manual (“shortest cable possible”). Maximum length is AWAITING BLUETTI.

Do not put the Epad in the battery box.

## Storage, before and after

Before: cabover volume, dinette seating, kitchen cabinets (undescribed), bath cabinet (undescribed), rear lift-bed cavity (undescribed), rotocast exterior bays (count and size unpublished), a pass-through flagged by RV Guide only.

After, if the batteries stay underbelly and out of the cabin:

- Cabover, dinette, and kitchen stay.
- One of bath cabinet or rear cavity is spent on the washer. The other can stay storage.
- The rear cavity is also the desk, if option 1 or 2 above is used. Desk plus washer plus storage in one cavity is the collision to avoid. Pick at most two of those three.
- One exterior bay may be spent if option f is used for the hub or a pack. D-09 assigns the remaining bays to tow gear, hoses, blocks, and tools, and leaves the assignment movable because the bays have no sizes yet.

No cubic-foot inventory is claimed. The before/after is a list of zones, not a volume.
