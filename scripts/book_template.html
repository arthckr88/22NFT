Holds

  • Six panels crosswise: 186.8 in in one row (15.6 ft), confirmed by the panel spec. Split around a mid-roof AC they take about 233 in of roof.
  • Parallel wiring: 3 × 13.69 A = 41.1 A per RV5 input, under the 50 A limit.
  • The battery box does not set the departure angle; the hitch receiver still bottoms first.
  • RV5 sits inside the desk pedestal, directly above the packs, with the kneehole open.

Changed

  • Tail battery axle shift: the earlier {{RC_ER}} lb rear / {{RC_EF}} lb front assumed the packs 80 in behind the axle. The modelled spot is 64 in behind it: {{RC_MR}} rear / {{RC_MF}} front for the 203 lb of packs.
  • Whole build: {{TA_F}} lb front / {{TA_R}} lb rear with laundry A, {{TB_F}} / {{TB_R}} with laundry B.
  • Starlink modelled as the Mini (40–50 W) beside the AC.

Measure first

  • Flat roof length and where the 14×14 AC opening sits.
  • Tail space: frame height, spare position, receiver height.
  • Per-axle weight of the loaded coach.

Axle math.
