# 22NFT Install Plan: Audit Packet

**Date:** October 7, 2026 (Pacific Time) · **Revision:** install map R5 (power centre in the lounge seat base)
**Purpose:** An outside reviewer should check this placement plan for physical fit, weight distribution, plumbing and electrical sense. Please be blunt. Section 8 lists the questions.

**Attachments:**
- `22NFT-install-map.pdf`: the four drawings (floor plan, side section, underbody, roof), the schedule and the fit checks.
- `factory-floorplan.png`: East to West's official 2027 22NF floorplan, which every interior placement is drawn on.

**Status tags:**
- **VERIFIED** means a manufacturer or retailer spec.
- **CREDIBLE** means a dealer or spec database.
- **ESTIMATE** means modelled or scaled, not measured.
- **UNVERIFIED** means not published.

Nothing is bought yet. Every position below is an ESTIMATE until a real coach is tape-measured.

## 1. Vehicle

- **Coach:** East to West Alita 22NF / 22NFT, a Class C on a Ford Transit 350 cutaway.
- **Size:** 26 ft 0 in long, 11 ft 5 in high, 178 in wheelbase. VERIFIED
- **Weight ratings:** GVWR 11,000 lb. GCWR 13,500 lb. GAWR front 4,630 lb, rear 7,275 lb. VERIFIED
- **Empty weight and axle split:** not published. One dealer unit's sticker read 8,976 lb dry and 1,765 lb cargo capacity (TEAM NOTE). One listing shows 9,031 lb (CREDIBLE).
- **Tanks:** fresh 35, grey 30, black 30 gal. VERIFIED. Their locations are UNVERIFIED.
- **Factory layout,** from the floorplan, front to rear:
  - cab with two swivel seats and a detachable swivel tabletop
  - driver-side lounge seat base forward of the bath
  - curbside entry door, flip-up counter and TV
  - curbside galley, fridge and pantry
  - driver-side dry bath: shower, toilet, lav
  - rear 60 × 80 lift bed, with a factory desk under it along the driver side
  - large cargo doors in the rear wall and on the curbside under the bed

## 2. Coordinate system used everywhere

- **x:** inches forward of the rear wall. The rear axle is at x 94 and the front axle at x 272 (ESTIMATE, from a 312 in overall length and the published 178 in wheelbase).
- **y:** inches from the centreline. Positive is the driver (roadside) side, negative is the curb side.
- **z:** inches above the ground. The floor is modelled at z 36.
- **How the floorplan was scaled:** the factory image was scaled to 4.9 px/in lengthwise, with the walls at ±43.5 in. Interior positions read off it are approximate.

## 5. Axle math

- **Method:** static lever arm. The front axle load change is w × (x − 94) / 178, and the rest goes to the rear axle.
- **Build only:** 684.4 lb in total, of which **+138.6 lb lands on the front axle and +545.8 lb on the rear**.

| Item | lb | x (in) | from rear axle (+ ahead) | front | rear |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 × B4810 packs, stacked in lounge base | 202.8 | 162.5 | +68.5 | +78.0 | +124.8 |
| Pack tray and straps (estimate) | 10.0 | 162.5 | +68.5 | +3.8 | +6.2 |
| RV5 hub, lounge base | 30.9 | 191.4 | +97.4 | +16.9 | +14.0 |
| Solar panels 1–3 (29.8 lb each) | 89.4 | 58.5 | -35.5 | -17.8 | +107.2 |
| Solar panels 4–6 (29.8 lb each) | 89.4 | 198.5 | +104.5 | +52.5 | +36.9 |
| Panel mounts, combiner, cable (estimate) | 60.0 | 128.0 | +34.0 | +11.5 | +48.5 |
| 2 × Orion-Tr, under passenger seat | 7.9 | 214.6 | +120.6 | +5.4 | +2.5 |
| 48 V, PV and AC cable runs (estimate) | 40.0 | 160.0 | +66.0 | +14.8 | +25.2 |

| Splendide WDV2200XCD washer-dryer | 148.0 | 60.6 | -33.4 | -27.7 | +175.7 |

**Loaded trip.** This adds the build, a full fresh tank (290.5 lb at x 138), a 40 lb tow bar on the receiver (x −6), a 200 lb driver (x 250) and 350 lb of gear. Empty axle weights are unpublished, so the last two columns show the heaviest each **empty** axle can be and still stay legal:

| Scenario | Added front | Added rear | Empty rear axle can be at most (7,275) | Empty front axle can be at most (4,630) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Panels 29.8 lb each, gear midship (x 130) | +434 | +1131 | 6,144 | 4,196 |
| Panels 29.8 lb each, gear rear garage (x 40) | +257 | +1308 | 5,967 | 4,373 |
| Panels 65.7 lb each, gear midship (x 130) | +476 | +1305 | 5,970 | 4,154 |
| Panels 65.7 lb each, gear rear garage (x 40) | +299 | +1482 | 5,793 | 4,331 |

- **What it means:** with an empty coach of about 9,000 lb, the rear axle stays legal if no more than about 64–68% of that weight sits on the rear axle. Only a CAT scale reading of the empty coach confirms it.
- **Towing:** GCWR 13,500 minus a 2,491–2,512 lb Fiat 500 Abarth means the coach must stay under about 10,988–11,009 lb, minus the tow kit.
- **Not included:** the Chill Cube swap is left out because the factory AC's weight is unpublished, and propane is left out because the LP capacity is listed as TBD.

**Electrical design:**
- **Solar:** six panels in parallel, three per RV5 input. That's 41.07 A short-circuit current per input against a 50 A limit. Each panel gets a 25 A fuse (the panel's max series fuse rating), and no blocking diodes are used.
- **Packs:** 2 AWG or larger, with a fuse at the pack terminals.
- **Shore power:** a hardwired surge protector / EMS on the shore inlet.
- **Generator:** charges in Standard or Silent mode, not Turbo.

## 7. Open fit checks (must pass on a real coach)

- **Lounge seat base.** Measure the inside of the driver-side lounge base: length (needs about 52 in for the stack, the 8 in gap and the RV5), depth from the wall (needs about 20 in for the RV5 plus the front-panel gear), and clear height (needs about 12.5 in for the stacked packs). Check what the factory put in there: furnace, water heater, tank or wiring.
- **RV5 airflow.** The manual asks for 7.87 in of open air at the RV5. Inside the seat base it gets 8 in on the side facing the packs and a louvred front panel; the end wall and front gap are closer. Confirm which faces vent, and add a small fan if needed.
- **Roof fans and skylight under the panels.** The panels sit 2.5 in off the roof, and the model puts two roof fans and the bath skylight under them. A MaxxFan Deluxe with rain dome is 23.2 × 16.6 in and stands 5.0 in tall closed and 9.1 in with the lid open (retailer spec, overall unit height). It cannot sit under a panel on 2.5 in brackets. See the roof fallback section.
- **Roof length and AC position.** The row of six spans 233 in from the first panel edge to the last (x 12 to 245). The model treats the roof as flat from the rear cap over the cab-over to about x 300. Measure the flat roof length, whether the cab-over roof is flat and walkable, and the AC opening’s distance from the rear cap.
- **Passenger seat swivel.** The cab seats swivel to face the table. Check what’s under the passenger swivel base and whether two Orions (each about 7.3 × 5.1 × 3.1 in) fit without blocking the swivel. Fallback: the floor beside the seat base.
- **Generator bay, shore inlet and breaker panel.** Find the generator bay, the shore inlet and the factory breaker panel. The 120 V feeds come in under the floor to the lounge base; their lengths depend on where those are.
- **Washer corner.** Measure the lift bed’s underside fully lowered at the forward curbside corner (needs at least 33.7 in), the floor space from the pantry wall back (needs 23½ in) and out from the curbside wall (needs 22⅝ in), where the bath and galley supply lines run, and where the grey tank inlet is.
- **Cargo doors.** The factory floorplan shows large cargo doors in the rear wall and on the curbside under the bed. The curbside door swings outward, so it still opens; the washer takes about 4½ in off the forward end of its roughly 33 in opening. Measure the opening.

## 8. Questions for the auditor

1. Check every placement against its space using the dimensions in section 4. Does anything not fit, or block a door, a walkway, a seat, the bed or service access?
2. Is the lounge seat base a sound home for 203 lb of LiFePO4 plus the RV5 inverter? Look at heat, airflow (the RV5 wants 7.87 in), floor loading, crash restraint and fire safety. What would you add, such as a fan, a thermal cut-off or a strap rating?
3. Check the axle math in section 5. Are the positions and arithmetic right, and are any loads missing?
4. Washer corner: is a vented Splendide at the curbside forward end of the lift bed sensible for plumbing (supply from the bath across the aisle, drain to grey), venting, and the bed clearance when it's lowered?
5. Electrical: three panels in parallel per input with 25 A fuses and no diodes, Orions charging the packs directly, and the transfer switch and surge protector in the same seat base as the packs. Any safety or code problems?
6. What's missing from this plan?

## 9. Known weak points (stated up front)

- **Floorplan scaling:** every interior position is scaled from the factory floorplan image, not measured.
- **Lounge base height:** the interior height isn't published, and the stacked packs need about 12.5 in.
- **RV5 airflow:** the full 7.87 in vent clearance isn't met on every face inside the seat base.
- **Washer clearance:** the lowered-bed clearance at the washer corner is unknown. It needs at least 33.7 in.
- **Underbody:** tank, generator and shore inlet locations are model guesses.
- **Panel weight:** unresolved, 29.8 lb or 29.8 kg each.
- **Out-of-date materials:** the 3D model and renders in PR #3 still show an earlier, wrong interior and the old tail battery box, so they shouldn't be used to judge placement. The earlier review packet (v3) is also out of date on battery, washer and switchgear locations.
