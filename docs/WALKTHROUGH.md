# Verify at walkthrough

Every rendered installation coordinate is ESTIMATE. Record measurements in inches, photograph the tape in place, note year/VIN and equipment labels. Take empty and realistic loaded axle scale tickets. The desk/drawers, kitchen, couch, lift bed, dry bath and cab-over sleeping function are preserved requirements.

## 1. Identity, payload and running gear
- [ ] Confirm model year, exact floorplan name 22NF/22NFT and VIN. Photograph OEM floorplan, certification label and OCCC label.
- [ ] Verify overall length, exterior width, height with current A/C, mirrors/awning protrusions, front/rear axle centers and 178 in wheelbase.
- [ ] Weigh front and rear axles empty, then with full fuel, intended fresh water, driver, camera equipment, luggage and scooter. Record load on each side if possible.
- [ ] Capture actual factory A/C, solar, cabinetry and battery masses to be removed. Gross additions in this book are not net payload consumption.
- [ ] Confirm hitch rating, coach actual loaded weight, GCWR margin, towbar/baseplate/braking requirements and exact Fiat year/transmission owner-manual flat-tow permission. No generic silhouette establishes towing approval.

## 2. Roof: highest priority
- [ ] Measure usable roof from cab-over transition to rear cap, full width at panel mounting height, crown curvature and roof structural attachment locations.
- [ ] Mark every A/C opening, vent, plumbing cap, antenna and cable gland in a scaled coordinate grid. Confirm actual opening and existing duct routes.
- [ ] Place six 68.35 × 30.16 in panel templates with mounting feet and roof-edge setback; test existing fixture positions before considering relocation.
- [ ] Check service clearance around 29.5 × 29 in heat pump, coil intake/exhaust, fasteners, return plenum and maintenance walkway. The modeled equipment band uses almost all estimated roof length.
- [ ] Verify panel NET mass from an individual panel and package label. Website 65.65 lb each is unresolved; check roof concentrated load and mounting engineering.
- [ ] Verify electrical label, cold Voc, fuse/conductor requirements, parallel branch routing and RV5 independent PV input limits. Six parallel produces 77.52 A operating / 82.14 A Isc.
- [ ] Confirm Starlink variant, mount size, 110° clear sky view, A/C shading, roof wire path and waterproof gland. Check lift-bed travel beneath ceiling controls.

## 3. Tail enclosure: highest priority
- [ ] Measure spare center/diameter/width, carrier travel and access; frame rails, crossmembers, tanks, drain valves, exhaust, generator, hitch, driveshaft and brake/fuel/water lines.
- [ ] Survey actual available L/W/H beside spare, using 45.5 × 37 × 9.5 in as a packaging TEST, not an approved target.
- [ ] Check ground clearance and rear overhang at loaded ride height, suspension compression, axle articulation and curb/ramp departure. Model 10.2 in / 5.9° is only a calculated estimate.
- [ ] Obtain manufacturer permission for B4810 mounting orientation/stacking and restraint. Check accessible terminal zones and minimum cooling/service clearances for RV5.
- [ ] Identify engineered frame attachment points and load paths; do not treat historical Ford generic subframe section as a motorhome approval.
- [ ] Design dry weatherproof ventilation, splash/stone protection, road-salt drainage, thermal management, cable strain relief, disconnect access and removable lid.
- [ ] Measure actual component CG positions and enclosure/bracket weights. Recalculate axle increments and front-axle loading after installation.

## 4. Laundry A versus B: highest priority
- [ ] Measure actual interior clear width and rear bay length; model lining suggests ~84 in while an older dealer/dossier value was 81 in. Do not use model aisle width as verified fit.
- [ ] Survey bath-adjacent storage bay A and opposite-desk garage bay B in all three dimensions, including wall protrusions/baseboard and floor strength.
- [ ] Mock appliance 23.5 × 22.625 × 33.375 in with 1 in front/back case clearances, hose connections, service removal route and ~40.5 in door-open depth.
- [ ] Trace the entire lift-bed swept volume with bed fully raised, lowered and partially lowered; verify neither appliance/door/vent nor gear blocks travel or emergency egress.
- [ ] Check retained desk drawer opening, chair workspace, camera-case/light-stand/luggage/scooter paths and rear garage access door.
- [ ] Confirm floor can support ≥280 lb dynamic appliance requirement with proper anchoring and vibration isolation; do not count this rating as the machine mass.
- [ ] Measure hot/cold tee access with individual shutoffs, hose serviceability and actual gray-branch size/slope/venting. Verify tank/branch can accept a 7 gpm pump discharge.
- [ ] Locate dedicated trapped standpipe ≥1.25 in ID, 25–34 in above appliance bottom, air break and trap service access. The book’s routes are topology, not an approved plumbing drawing.
- [ ] Measure dedicated 4 in metal dryer vent to exterior with permitted bends/length, weather hood, downslope where specified and clearance from exhaust/windows/combustibles. No common bathroom/roof duct.
- [ ] Verify enclosure makeup air ≥8 sq in and actual access door/grille. Confirm dedicated 120 V 15 A branch, 11 A/1,300 W peak, startup and simultaneous load budget.
- [ ] Run an actual wash/dry cycle to measure water use, kWh, drying duration, vibration/noise at the desk and lift bed. Scenario water/energy figures are estimates.

## 5. Charging, HVAC and quiet hours
- [ ] Confirm Ford CCP rating, alternator options, running detection, wiring/fusing and current-year upfitter restrictions with the VIN.
- [ ] Confirm Orion-Tr Smart exact model, thermal spacing, forward dry mounting/service access and Bluetti permission for external 48 V charging/BMS integration. Never route up-to-60 V Orion output into a 50 V-max PV input.
- [ ] Identify generator make/model/fuel/start/stop connector and rated usable output. Confirm safe AGS controller/relay interface, SOC trigger, quiet-hour inhibit and battery-sufficient start supply; no stationary chassis-engine auto-start.
- [ ] Confirm RV5 transfer/neutral-ground behavior and generator AC input compatibility, 12 V house distribution and HVAC/laundry branch capacities.
- [ ] Verify heat-pump ducted SKU, thermostat/ADB/controller compatibility, return opening, condensate path, roof loading and lift-bed/head interference.
- [ ] Measure actual 100°F cooling demand, indoor temperature/humidity and compressor draw over time; sustained cooling is not established by solar wattage alone.
- [ ] Log editing, refrigerator, Starlink and overnight loads to set real SOC reserve and AGS thresholds.
