## Proposed configurations / calculations

- Landscape panel rows use 15.50 ft with one-inch gaps; modeled array + 29.5 in A/C band uses 18.04 ft (216.46 in), before real service setbacks, on a 220 in usable-roof ESTIMATE.
- Six parallel: 77.52 A Imp / 82.14 A Isc, conflicting with published 50 A RV5 input. Split 3+3 parallel inputs remain an alternative requiring manual confirmation, not a finalized change.
- 12/48-8 Orion Smart pair chosen provisionally; up-to-60 V output cannot feed RV5 50 V-max PV. External B4810 charge acceptance/BMS path and Ford CCP need confirmation.
- Tail bare-envelope study 45.5 × 37 × 9.5 in with side-by-side packs; cooling/service/mounting margins unresolved. Ground clearance ~10.2 in / departure ~5.9° ESTIMATE.
- Complete tail assembly 288.66 lb including hub and 55 lb enclosure/cable allowances: Δrear +403.45 / Δfront −114.79 lb at estimated CGs. Old +294/−91 was battery pair alone near an 80 in lever.
- Conservative gross additions 998.64 lb with one laundry option, before actual removed hardware and ordinary carrying load. Remaining payload unknown. Website panel mass 65.65 lb each is a net-mass verification gate.
- Usable AC energy scenario 7.37 kWh (80% SOC window ×90% inverter efficiency). Moderate daily demand ~7.38 kWh vs ~6.81 solar. 100°F stress scenario ~31.78 kWh/day vs ~6.93 solar, requiring ~8–11 h/day generator equivalent. Solar harvest assumes an approved input arrangement can accept the full array; one 50 A input at 21.29 V has an approximate 1.06 kW ceiling. Actual clipping needs an irradiance/input model. No continuous-cooling promise.
